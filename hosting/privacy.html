SmartAgro Connect

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 December 2026

In short

  • We collect what the marketplace needs to work: your account details, your listings, your chats and your orders.
  • Card and bank payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card details, and we keep only a masked bank account number (for example ******6789).
  • Buyers and sellers in an order can see each other's name and phone number so they can arrange delivery.
  • If you request transport, transporters can see the pickup and drop-off places. While the delivery is under way, the buyer and seller can see the transporter's live location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

SmartAgro Connect ("SmartAgro", "we", "us") is a marketplace app that connects farmers, buyers and transporters in Nigeria. It is operated by SmartAgroConnect, No. 280 Federal Low-cost Area, Gashua, Yobe State, Nigeria. We are the data controller for the personal data described here, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Contact for privacy questions: smartagroconnect@gmail.com.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the marketplace: listings, search, chat, orders, delivery tracking and ratings.
  • To take payments and pay sellers (through Paystack).
  • To send notifications you'd expect, such as new messages and order updates.
  • To keep the platform safe: preventing fraud, enforcing our Terms of Use, and handling reports.
  • To fix problems and improve the app, using crash reports and basic usage statistics.

Our legal bases under the NDPA are: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a safe marketplace, complying with the law, and your consent where we ask for it (for example notifications or location).

4. Who we share it with

  • The people you trade with. In an order, the buyer and seller can see each other's name and phone number. Your listings, name and seller rating are visible to signed-in users.
  • Paystack processes card and bank payments and pays sellers. Their privacy policy applies to the payment details you give them.
  • Google Firebase hosts our database, files, sign-in, notifications, crash reports and analytics. Data may be stored and processed outside Nigeria (for example in the United States) with appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud or harm.

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: while your account is open, and up to 12 months after you close it.
  • Order and payment records: 6 years, for tax and accounting.
  • Chats, photos and voice notes: while your account is open, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: up to 90 days in identifiable form.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data (we may keep order and payment records we are legally required to keep);
  • stop or limit some uses, or withdraw consent you gave;
  • move your data to another service.

Email smartagroconnect@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by strict security rules (for example, only the buyer and seller can see an order), and payment secrets are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.

8. Children

SmartAgro is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will show the new date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
